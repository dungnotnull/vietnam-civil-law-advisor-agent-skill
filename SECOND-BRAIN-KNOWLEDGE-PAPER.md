## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. National Assembly of Vietnam (2015). Civil Code No. 91/2015/QH13. Vietnam — Primary statutory source governing Vietnamese civil law.
2. Pham Duy Nghia (2011). Vietnamese Business Law in Transition. National Political Publishing House — Overview of Vietnam's civil/commercial legal transition.
3. J. Gillespie (2006). Transplanting Commercial Law Reform: Developing a 'Rule of Law' in Vietnam. Ashgate — Analysis of civil-law transplantation into Vietnam's legal system.
4. K. Zweigert, H. Kötz (1998). An Introduction to Comparative Law (3rd ed.). Oxford University Press — Comparative civil-law methodology underlying code-based reasoning.
5. Nguyen Ngoc Dien (2017). Textbook on Civil Law (Vietnamese Civil Code Commentary). Ho Chi Minh City National University Press — Vietnamese academic commentary on the Civil Code.
6. J. Gillespie, P. Nicholson (2005). Asian Socialism and Legal Change: The Dynamics of Vietnamese and Chinese Reform. ANU Press — Comparative socialist-civil-law reform analysis.
7. B. Kritzer (2012). Legal Systems of the World: A Political, Social, and Cultural Encyclopedia. ABC-CLIO — Reference for civil-law family classification relevant to Vietnam.
8. P. Nicholson, S. Pham Duy Nghia (eds.) (2008). Vietnamese Legal Culture. In Legal Reform in Developing and Transitional States — Contextual overview of Vietnamese legal reasoning norms.
9. National Assembly of Vietnam (2013). Land Law No. 45/2013/QH13. Vietnam — Governs property/land rights intersecting with Civil Code.
10. National Assembly of Vietnam (2014). Law on Marriage and Family No. 52/2014/QH13. Vietnam — Governs family-law matters referenced by the Civil Code.
11. Supreme People's Court of Vietnam. Selected Precedent (Án lệ) Collections. — Official precedent guidance supplementing code-based reasoning.
12. R. B. Cappalli (2002). The Disappearance of Legal Method. Temple Law Review — General discussion of IRAC/legal-memo methodology.
13. H. P. Glenn (2014). Legal Traditions of the World (5th ed.). Oxford University Press — Comparative framework situating Vietnam's civil-law tradition.
14. World Bank (2019). Doing Business: Vietnam Economy Profile. World Bank Group — Empirical overview of contract-enforcement practice in Vietnam.
15. Do Van Dai (2016). Comments on Vietnamese Civil Law on Contracts and Civil Liability. Hong Duc Publishing House — Academic commentary on contract/tort provisions.
16. J. H. Merryman, R. Pérez-Perdomo (2007). The Civil Law Tradition (3rd ed.). Stanford University Press — Foundational reference on civil-law reasoning method.
17. Ngo Huy Cuong (2009). Comparative Civil Law: Vietnam and Other Jurisdictions. Vietnam National University Press — Comparative analysis relevant to code interpretation.
18. P. Nicholson (2007). Borrowing Court Systems: The Experience of Socialist Vietnam. Martinus Nijhoff — Institutional context for Vietnamese civil-law adjudication.
19. National Assembly of Vietnam (2015). Civil Procedure Code No. 92/2015/QH13. Vietnam — Procedural framework for civil-law claims.
20. K. Taylor (2013). A History of the Vietnamese. Cambridge University Press — Historical/cultural context relevant to understanding Vietnam's legal development.
