# Property and Land Rights Checklist

Comprehensive issue-spotting and validation checklist for property and land rights matters under Vietnamese Civil Code 2015 and Land Law 2013. Use this checklist when analyzing ownership, land use rights, transfers, disputes, or other property matters.

## Checklist Overview

This checklist operationalizes the civil-law code-based syllogistic reasoning methodology for property analysis. Each item represents a legal requirement derived from Civil Code 2015 articles and Land Law 2013 provisions.

**Dual Citation Format:** This checklist requires dual citation format:
- **CC2015**: Civil Code 2015
- **LL2013**: Land Law 2013

## Part 1: Ownership Rights

### 1.1 Establishment of Ownership

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 189-200: Establishment of ownership rights
- Land Law References: Articles 5-15: Land allocation and lease

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Ownership Grounds**: Is ownership properly established?
  - Creation of property (CC2015, Art. 189)
  - Transfer of ownership rights (CC2015, Art. 190)
  - Inheritance (CC2015, Art. 191)
  - Findings of property (CC2015, Art. 192)
  - Other legal grounds (CC2015, Art. 193)

- [ ] **Proof of Ownership**: Is ownership properly documented?
  - Title certificate/land use right certificate (LL2013, Art. 23)
  - Registration documents (CC2015, Art. 194)
  - Contract of transfer (CC2015, Art. 195)
  - Court decision or administrative decision (CC2015, Art. 196)

- [ ] **Ownership Type**: What type of ownership applies?
  - State ownership (CC2015, Art. 197)
  - Collective ownership (CC2015, Art. 198)
  - Private ownership (CC2015, Art. 199)
  - Mixed ownership (CC2015, Art. 200)

**Common Issues:**
- Unclear ownership establishment
- Missing or invalid title documents
- Disputed ownership claims
- Improper ownership transfers

### 1.2 Ownership Rights Contents

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 201-210: Contents of ownership rights

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Possession Rights**: Are possession rights clear?
  - Right to possess property (CC2015, Art. 201)
  - Exclusive possession (CC2015, Art. 202)

- [ ] **Use Rights**: Are use rights properly defined?
  - Right to use property (CC2015, Art. 203)
  - Right to income and fruits (CC2015, Art. 204)
  - Right to exploit resources (CC2015, Art. 205)

- [ ] **Disposition Rights**: Are disposition rights clear?
  - Right to transfer ownership (CC2015, Art. 206)
  - Right to exchange property (CC2015, Art. 207)
  - Right to gift property (CC2015, Art. 208)
  - Right to mortgage property (CC2015, Art. 209)
  - Right to destroy property (CC2015, Art. 210)

**Common Issues:**
- Unclear scope of use rights
- Restrictions on disposition rights
- Conflicts between ownership types
- Limitations on property exploitation

## Part 2: Land Use Rights

### 2.1 Land Allocation and Lease

**Land Law References:**
- Articles 16-30: Land allocation
- Articles 31-45: Land lease

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Land Allocation**: Is land allocation proper?
  - Eligibility for allocation (LL2013, Art. 17)
  - Allocation procedures followed (LL2013, Art. 18)
  - Allocation decision issued (LL2013, Art. 19)
  - Land use right certificate obtained (LL2013, Art. 23)

- [ ] **Land Lease**: Is land lease proper?
  - Lease eligibility verified (LL2013, Art. 32)
  - Lease terms established (LL2013, Art. 33)
  - Lease duration specified (LL2013, Art. 34)
  - Lease payments determined (LL2013, Art. 35)

- [ ] **Land Use Purpose**: Is land use purpose lawful?
  - Purpose matches land use planning (LL2013, Art. 10)
  - Purpose complies with land use regulations
  - Purpose changes properly authorized

**Common Issues:**
- Improper land allocation procedures
- Missing land use right certificates
- Non-compliance with land use planning
- Unauthorized purpose changes

### 2.2 Land Use Right Certificate

**Land Law References:**
- Articles 20-25: Land use right certificates

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Certificate Validity**: Is the certificate valid?
  - Certificate issued by competent authority (LL2013, Art. 21)
  - Certificate properly registered (LL2013, Art. 22)
  - Certificate information accurate (LL2013, Art. 23)
  - Certificate not expired or revoked

- [ ] **Certificate Information**: Is certificate information complete?
  - Land parcel identification (LL2013, Art. 23)
  - Land user information (LL2013, Art. 23)
  - Land use purpose specified (LL2013, Art. 23)
  - Land use duration stated (LL2013, Art. 23)
  - Land use rights specified (LL2013, Art. 23)

- [ ] **Certificate Transfer**: Can certificate be transferred?
  - Transferability verified (LL2013, Art. 24)
  - Transfer procedures followed (LL2013, Art. 24)
  - Transfer limitations considered (LL2015, Art. 25)

**Common Issues:**
- Invalid certificates
- Inaccurate certificate information
- Improper certificate transfers
- Missing certificates

### 2.3 Land Use Rights for Foreign Entities

**Land Law References:**
- Articles 159-165: Land use by foreign organizations

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Foreign Entity Eligibility**: Is foreign entity eligible?
  - Investment project approved (LL2013, Art. 160)
  - Investment certificate obtained (LL2013, Art. 161)
  - Foreign organization properly registered

- [ ] **Land Use Rights**: What land use rights apply?
  - Land lease rights (LL2013, Art. 162)
  - Rights limited to project duration (LL2013, Art. 163)
  - No ownership rights (LL2013, Art. 164)

- [ ] **Special Provisions**: Are special provisions applicable?
  - Foreign investment zone considerations
  - Special economic zone provisions
  - Industry-specific restrictions

**Common Issues:**
- Foreign entity ineligibility
- Misunderstanding of rights (lease vs. ownership)
- Investment project issues
- Regulatory non-compliance

## Part 3: Property Transactions

### 3.1 Transfer of Ownership

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 438-456: Transfer of ownership

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Transfer Capacity**: Do parties have capacity to transfer?
  - Transferor has ownership rights (CC2015, Art. 438)
  - Transferee has capacity to receive (CC2015, Art. 439)
  - No restrictions on transfer (CC2015, Art. 440)

- [ ] **Transfer Formalities**: Are formalities completed?
  - Written contract executed (CC2015, Art. 441)
  - Notarization completed (LL2013, Art. 24)
  - Registration completed (LL2013, Art. 25)
  - Tax obligations satisfied (CC2015, Art. 442)

- [ ] **Transfer Conditions**: Are transfer conditions met?
  - Price/payment terms agreed (CC2015, Art. 443)
  - Delivery conditions specified (CC2015, Art. 444)
  - Risk transfer point defined (CC2015, Art. 445)

**Common Issues:**
- Missing transfer capacity
- Incomplete formalities
- Unclear transfer conditions
- Tax non-compliance

### 3.2 Mortgage and Security Interests

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 292-310: Mortgage
- Land Law References: Articles 108-115: Mortgage of land use rights

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Mortgage Capacity**: Can property be mortgaged?
  - Ownership rights established (CC2015, Art. 292)
  - No restrictions on mortgage (CC2015, Art. 293)
  - Property within mortgageable categories (LL2013, Art. 108)

- [ ] **Mortgage Formalities**: Are formalities completed?
  - Mortgage contract executed (CC2015, Art. 294)
  - Mortgage registration completed (CC2015, Art. 295)
  - Certificate notation made (LL2013, Art. 110)

- [ ] **Mortgage Enforcement**: Can mortgage be enforced?
  - Default conditions met (CC2015, Art. 296)
  - Enforcement procedures available (CC2015, Art. 297)
  - Priority of mortgage established (CC2015, Art. 298)

**Common Issues:**
- Improper mortgage capacity
- Missing mortgage registration
- Unclear enforcement procedures
- Priority disputes

**Common Issues:**

## Part 5: Special Property Matters

### 5.1 Co-ownership

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 213-231: Co-ownership

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Co-ownership Establishment**: Is co-ownership proper?
  - Co-ownership type identified (CC2015, Art. 213)
  - Co-ownership shares defined (CC2015, Art. 214)
  - Co-ownership agreement in place (CC2015, Art. 215)

- [ ] **Co-owner Rights**: What rights do co-owners have?
  - Use and enjoyment rights (CC2015, Art. 216)
  - Income sharing rights (CC2015, Art. 217)
  - Disposition rights (CC2015, Art. 218)
  - First refusal rights (CC2015, Art. 219)

- [ ] **Partition**: Can co-ownership be partitioned?
  - Partition availability (CC2015, Art. 220)
  - Partition procedures (CC2015, Art. 221)
  - Partition disputes (CC2015, Art. 222)

**Common Issues:**
- Unclear co-ownership shares
- Disputes over use rights
- Partition conflicts
- First right of refusal disputes

### 5.2 Finding and Buried Treasure

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 192-193: Findings of property

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Finding Conditions**: Are finding conditions met?
  - Property found without owner (CC2015, Art. 192)
  - Reporting requirements met (CC2015, Art. 193)
  - Ownership claim period expired (CC2015, Art. 194)

- [ ] **Buried Treasure**: Does buried treasure provision apply?
  - Treasure definition met (CC2015, Art. 195)
  - Ownership determination (CC2015, Art. 196)
  - Reward for finder (CC2015, Art. 197)

**Common Issues:**
- Unclear ownership of found property
- Disputed buried treasure claims
- Reward disputes
- Failure to report findings
