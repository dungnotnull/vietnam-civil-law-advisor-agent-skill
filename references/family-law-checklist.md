# Family Law and Inheritance Checklist

Comprehensive issue-spotting and validation checklist for family law and inheritance matters under Vietnamese Civil Code 2015 and Marriage and Family Law 2014. Use this checklist when analyzing marriage, divorce, succession, wills, or family property matters.

## Checklist Overview

This checklist operationalizes the civil-law code-based syllogistic reasoning methodology for family law and inheritance analysis. Each item represents a legal requirement derived from Civil Code 2015 articles and Marriage and Family Law 2014 provisions.

**Dual Citation Format:** This checklist requires dual citation format:
- **CC2015**: Civil Code 2015
- **MFL2014**: Marriage and Family Law 2014

## Part 1: Marriage and Family Relations

### 1.1 Marriage Formation

**Marriage and Family Law References:**
- Articles 21-35: Conditions for marriage
- Articles 36-45: Marriage registration

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Marriage Capacity**: Do parties have capacity to marry?
  - Age requirement: Male 20+, Female 18+ (MFL2014, Art. 21)
  - Voluntary consent (MFL2014, Art. 22)
  - Full civil act capacity (MFL2014, Art. 23)
  - No prohibitions on marriage (MFL2014, Art. 24)

- [ ] **Marriage Prohibitions**: Are there prohibited relationships?
  - Consanguinity restrictions (MFL2014, Art. 24)
  - Affinity restrictions (MFL2014, Art. 24)
  - Prior existing marriage (MFL2014, Art. 25)

- [ ] **Marriage Formalities**: Are formalities completed?
  - Marriage registration completed (MFL2014, Art. 36)
  - Certificate of marriage obtained (MFL2014, Art. 37)
  - Foreign marriage recognition (if applicable) (MFL2014, Art. 38)

**Common Issues:**
- Underage marriage
- Lack of voluntary consent
- Prohibited relationships
- Unregistered marriages

### 1.2 Husband and Wife Rights and Obligations

**Marriage and Family Law References:**
- Articles 46-55: Rights and obligations of husband and wife

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Property Regime**: What property regime applies?
  - Default regime: Separate property (MFL2014, Art. 46)
  - Agreed regime: Property agreement (MFL2014, Art. 47)
  - Agreement validity requirements (MFL2014, Art. 48)

- [ ] **Common Property**: What constitutes common property?
  - Property formed during marriage (MFL2014, Art. 49)
  - Income from separate property (MFL2014, Art. 50)
  - Gifts to both spouses (MFL2014, Art. 51)

- [ ] **Separate Property**: What constitutes separate property?
  - Property owned before marriage (MFL2014, Art. 52)
  - Property inherited/gifted to individual spouse (MFL2014, Art. 53)
  - Property from separate property (MFL2014, Art. 54)

- [ ] **Property Rights**: What property rights exist?
  - Right to use common property (MFL2014, Art. 55)
  - Right to manage common property (MFL2014, Art. 56)
  - Right to dispose common property (MFL2014, Art. 57)

**Common Issues:**
- Unclear property regime
- Disputed property classification
- Improper property agreements
- Disputes over property disposition

### 1.3 Divorce

**Marriage and Family Law References:**
- Articles 56-68: Divorce grounds
- Articles 69-75: Divorce procedures

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Divorce Grounds**: What grounds for divorce exist?
  - Mutual consent (MFL2014, Art. 56)
  - Court determination (MFL2014, Art. 57)
  - Specific grounds listed (MFL2014, Art. 58-62)

- [ ] **Divorce Procedures**: What procedures apply?
  - Administrative divorce (MFL2014, Art. 69)
  - Litigation divorce (MFL2014, Art. 70)
  - Jurisdiction considerations (MFL2014, Art. 71)

- [ ] **Divorce Consequences**: What are divorce consequences?
  - Property division (MFL2014, Art. 72)
  - Child custody (MFL2014, Art. 73)
  - Child support (MFL2014, Art. 74)
  - Spousal support (MFL2014, Art. 75)

**Common Issues:**
- Disputed divorce grounds
- Complex property division
- Custody disputes
- Support calculation issues

## Part 3: Inheritance Law

### 3.1 Testamentary Succession

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 464-498: Wills and testamentary disposition

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Will Capacity**: Does testator have capacity?
  - Full civil act capacity (CC2015, Art. 464)
  - Sound mind (CC2015, Art. 465)
  - No coercion or fraud (CC2015, Art. 466)

- [ ] **Will Form**: Is will form proper?
  - Written will (CC2015, Art. 467)
  - Notarized will (CC2015, Art. 468)
  - Handwritten will (CC2015, Art. 469)
  - Oral will (limited circumstances) (CC2015, Art. 470)

- [ ] **Will Contents**: Are will contents proper?
  - Identification of estate (CC2015, Art. 471)
  - Identification of beneficiaries (CC2015, Art. 472)
  - Disposition of estate (CC2015, Art. 473)
  - Appointment of executor (CC2015, Art. 474)

- [ ] **Will Validity**: Is will valid?
  - Formal requirements met (CC2015, Art. 475)
  - No illegal provisions (CC2015, Art. 476)
  - No violation of mandatory portions (CC2015, Art. 477)

**Common Issues:**
- Capacity challenges
- Formality defects
- Ambiguous provisions
- Mandatory portion violations

### 3.2 Legal Succession

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 499-512: Legal inheritance

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Heir Identification**: Who are the heirs?
  - First rank heirs (CC2015, Art. 501)
  - Second rank heirs (CC2015, Art. 502)
  - Third rank heirs (CC2015, Art. 503)
  - Fourth rank heirs (CC2015, Art. 504)

- [ ] **Inheritance Order**: What is inheritance order?
  - First rank inherits first (CC2015, Art. 505)
  - Subsequent ranks inherit only if prior rank doesn't exist (CC2015, Art. 506)
  - Same rank heirs inherit equally (CC2015, Art. 507)

- [ ] **Inheritance by Right of Representation**: Does representation apply?
  - Representation availability (CC2015, Art. 508)
  - Representation conditions (CC2015, Art. 509)
  - Representation effects (CC2015, Art. 510)

**Common Issues:**
- Heir identification disputes
- Inheritance order disputes
- Representation disputes
- Disinheritance challenges

### 3.3 Estate and Succession

**Civil Code References:**
- Articles 457-463: Estate and succession

**Checklist Items:**
- [ ] **Estate Composition**: What constitutes estate?
  - Property of deceased (CC2015, Art. 460)
  - Rights and obligations (CC2015, Art. 461)
  - Exclusions from estate (CC2015, Art. 462)

- [ ] **Opening of Succession**: When does succession open?
  - Time of opening (CC2015, Art. 457)
  - Place of opening (CC2015, Art. 458)
  - Presumption of death (CC2015, Art. 459)

- [ ] **Succession Exclusions**: Who is excluded from succession?
  - Persons who unlawfully caused death (CC2015, Art. 463)
  - Persons who seriously violated obligations (CC2015, Art. 463)
  - Other grounds for exclusion (CC2015, Art. 463)

**Common Issues:**
- Estate composition disputes
- Succession opening disputes
- Exclusion disputes
- Estate debt issues

**Civil Code References:**

**Checklist Items:**

- [ ] **Division Methods**: What division methods exist?

- [ ] **Special Considerations**: What special considerations apply?

**Common Issues:**
- Division method disputes
- Equality vs. equity disputes
- Family home disputes
