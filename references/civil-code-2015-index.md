## Key Articles for Common Legal Issues

### Contract Validity (Critical for Contract Analysis)
- **Article 117**: Conditions for civil transactions to be valid
  - Parties have full civil act capacity
  - Voluntary participation
  - Purpose and content not prohibited by law
  - Form not contrary to legal requirements

### Civil Liability Framework (Critical for Tort Analysis)
- **Article 584**: Grounds for civil liability
  - Illegal act
  - Damage occurred
  - Causal relationship between act and damage
  - Fault (intentional or negligent)
